Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy describes how the app Tiny Log: Baby Care ("the app") handles information.

Summary

The app stores everything you enter on your own device. We do not run a server for the app, we do not collect your data, and we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

What you enter

You may enter baby names, birth dates, feeding, sleep, diaper, play, growth and medicine entries, notes, and optional photos. This information is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. It is not sent to us or to any third party.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

Accounts and sharing

The app can be used without an account. Account and caregiver-sharing screens in this version work locally on your device only; no data is uploaded.

Third-party services

The app loads its typeface (Figtree) from Google Fonts when it starts, which means Google may receive your device's IP address. The app does not send any other data to third parties.

Children

The app is designed for parents and caregivers, not for children. It records information about babies entered by an adult, and keeps it on that adult's device.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app, or using the delete options inside it, removes the data stored on your device.

Not medical advice

The app is not a medical device. Charts and hints are for reference only; consult your doctor.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
