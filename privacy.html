Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy describes how the app Tiny Log: Baby Care ("the app") handles information.

Summary

The app works fully on your device without an account. If you choose to sign in with Apple, your entries are also backed up to our server so you can restore them and share them with family. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking, and we never sell data.

What you enter

You may enter baby names, birth dates, feeding, sleep, diaper, play, growth and medicine entries, appointments, notes, and optional photos. Without an account this stays on your device. With an account, the same entries are stored on our server (hosted on Railway, in a Postgres database) linked to your account.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

Accounts and sharing

Signing in is optional and uses Sign in with Apple. We receive a stable Apple account identifier and, if you share it, your name and email (or Apple’s private relay address). With an account you can invite family members using a one-time invite code; people who join see and add to the same baby log, and can leave at any time. Account holders can remove members. You can delete your account in the app (More → Me → Delete account), which deletes your account and your server data.

Third-party services

The app loads its typeface (Jost) from Google Fonts when it starts, which means Google may receive your device's IP address. The app does not send any other data to third parties.

Children

The app is designed for parents and caregivers, not for children. It records information about babies entered by an adult, and stores it on that adult's device and, if they sign in, on our server.

Live Activities

Running timers can show on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island. This is handled on your device; nothing is sent to us.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes data on your device. If you have an account, delete it inside the app to remove your server data as well.

Not medical advice

The app is not a medical device. Charts and hints are for reference only; consult your doctor.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
